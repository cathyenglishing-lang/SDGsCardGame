- **A2**（26 字 / 每句 8.7 字 / 約 16.4 秒）

- **B1**（34 字 / 每句 17.0 字 / 約 18.5 秒）

- **B2**（37 字 / 每句 18.5 字 / 約 18.5 秒）
  > Taipei and Taichung are being retrofitted as sponge cities. Impermeable concrete is gradually replaced by porous surfacing, while parks are engineered as retention basins, deliberately sunk so a deluge collects there rather than in the surrounding streets.
