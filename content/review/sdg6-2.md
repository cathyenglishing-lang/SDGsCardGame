# sdg6-2 · The Chip Industry's Thirst: Semiconductors and Water Scarcity

SDG 6 · 20 slides · levels: A2, B1, B2 · content_version 2026.09.1

> 原始工作表的任務敘事（原文保留）：
>
> Taiwan makes about 90% of the world's most advanced chips while also being one of the most water-stressed places on Earth. This talk traces that collision through TSMC's rising water use, the 2021 drought, genuine recycling and reclaiming progress, and the unresolved trade-off between industry and agriculture as droughts grow harder to predict.
