- **B1**（28 字 / 每句 14.0 字 / 15.3 秒 ·（本版新寫））

## Part 2 · Problem（問題指認：趨勢與因果）

### 4. Steep mountains, short rivers（地勢陡峭河川短促）

- **A2**（31 字 / 每句 10.3 字 / 19.6 秒 ·（本版新寫））
  > Taiwan's mountains are steep, and its rivers are short. So rain runs fast into the sea. Most rain falls between May and October, so the rest of the year is dry.
- **B1**（31 字 / 每句 15.5 字 / 16.9 秒 ·（本版新寫））
  > Because our mountains are steep and our rivers are short, rainwater rushes into the ocean before engineers can store it. Most of that rain falls between May and October each year.
- **B2**（37 字 / 每句 18.5 字 / 18.5 秒 ·（本版新寫））
  > Because the terrain is steep and the rivers are short, rainwater reaching the coast is largely lost to the ocean within hours. Concentrated mostly in the May-to-October wet season, that rainfall leaves the remaining months comparatively parched.

- **A2**（29 字 / 每句 9.7 字 / 18.3 秒 ·（本版新寫））

- **B2**（40 字 / 每句 20.0 字 / 20.0 秒 ·（本版新寫））

### 6. Mud is quietly stealing capacity（泥沙悄悄侵蝕庫容）

- **B1**（31 字 / 每句 15.5 字 / 16.9 秒 ·（本版新寫））

- **B1**（28 字 / 每句 14.0 字 / 15.3 秒 ·（本版新寫））

- **B1**（31 字 / 每句 15.5 字 / 16.9 秒 ·（本版新寫））
